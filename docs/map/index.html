About this data

A curated list, not a registry. Each project was read from news coverage, a company release, or local reporting, and lists the sources it came from. There is no authoritative public registry of these projects to draw from, so a project missing here may simply not have been found.

Locations are approximate. A marker sits at or near the named site where one was reported, otherwise within the township, borough or city. Statuses change quickly, so each project shows the date of the reporting behind it. A hollow marker means the project was denied or abandoned.

Figures are not comparable. Some entries report a power agreement, some a power plant's output, some floor area or investment. They are shown as reported and never added together.

Boundaries. Utility territories come from HIFLD's retail service territory layer, republished by Penn State's PASDA, and are approximate. County lines are derived from U.S. Census data.
