About this data

A curated list, not a registry. Each project was read from news coverage, a company release, or local reporting, and lists the sources it came from. There is no authoritative public registry of these projects to draw from, so a project missing here may simply not have been found.

Statuses. A project is Announced, Under local review, Approved for a first phase, Under construction, Rejected locally, or Abandoned. Each stage has its own marker shape as well as a color. Statuses change quickly, so each project shows the date of the reporting behind it, and an announcement that has had no news since is still listed as announced.

Who lives nearby. For projects placed at a specific site, the panel counts people and homes within half a mile to five miles of the marker, using 2020 Census block counts, and lists schools, hospitals, nursing homes and child care facilities within a mile. Existing housing only: planned developments are not counted. Census blocks are assigned by their center point, so counts near a ring edge are approximate, and the Census adds noise to small blocks to protect privacy. Projects located only to a township, borough or city show “not measured”, because that is too coarse to measure distance from.

Proximity is not impact. These figures describe who lives near a marker. They are not a forecast of noise, water use, emissions or rates for anyone, and the map has no risk score. Distances run from the marker, which is placed from reporting and is not a surveyed property line.

Local response. Notes on public comment, votes and opposition are included only where a source read for this map reported them, with a link. Where the panel says nothing was found, that means the sources read did not cover it.

Power plants. The optional layer shows operating plants of 100 MW or more from HIFLD, a federal layer that can lag reality. Where a project is reported to be at a named plant, its marker is placed there.

Figures are not comparable. Some entries report a power agreement, some a power plant's output, some floor area or investment. They are shown as reported and never added together.

Boundaries and sources. Utility territories, power plants and facilities come from HIFLD, republished by Penn State's PASDA, and are approximate. County lines are derived from U.S. Census data. Population and housing counts are from the 2020 Census redistricting files; blocks in New Jersey, Maryland and other neighboring states are included for projects near the state line.
